# Tilt / Anger Meter

The chalk bag clap manages anger after a bad putt, which implies an anger mechanic.

*(suggestions)*
- Bad shots raise tilt. High tilt hurts Touch and Read and adds variance to the next throw.
- Ways to reduce tilt: chalk clap, Nerve stat, a few skills, a snack (consumable).
- At max tilt the character throws a disc in anger, with a chance of losing it.
