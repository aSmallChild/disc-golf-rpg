# Shot Types

- **Spike hyzer**: steep high arc. Chance to clear tall obstacles. Can **tombstone** (stick vertically in the ground), so the disc won't roll down a slope.

*(suggestions)*
- **Tombstone** as its own outcome. Likelier on soft ground, impossible on ice or rock.
- **Roller**: big distance on hard or dry ground, terrible in tall grass, very risky near slopes and water.
- **Turbo putt / thumber / tomahawk**: overhand throws for getting over or around obstacles. Low accuracy, unlocked via the skill tree.
- **Skip shot**: bounces off hard ground or ice. Great on ice, useless in tall grass.
- **Hyzer flip / anhyzer**: curve around obstacles.
- **Straddle putt**: lower range, better stability in wind or awkward stances.
- **Scramble shot**: used from bad lies (bushes, behind trees). Gets its own skill.
