# Weather

Wind, rain, and snow influence skill rolls.

*(suggestions)*
- **Wind**: direction and strength. Headwind makes discs turn over, tailwind makes them fade more. Gusts add variance.
- **Rain**: grip penalty, less roll, and ground becomes softer (more tombstones).
- **Snow**: discs vanish on landing (find-disc roll), hardly any roll, cold reduces Arm.
- **Heat**: stamina drain.
- **Fog**: Read penalty, targets are harder to see.
