# Multiplayer

It would be good to have multiplayer.

*(suggestions)*
- Turn-based fits the game well, since there is no real-time action and latency doesn't matter.
- Modes: async play (take your turn whenever, like correspondence chess), live card of 2–4 players, async tournaments with leaderboards.
- The server is authoritative for dice rolls to prevent cheating.
