# Ground Surfaces

The type of ground changes how the disc reacts.

| Surface | *(suggested)* behaviour |
|---|---|
| Water | Disc may sink or float (disc type matters). Retrieval roll needed. Skip-shot chance. |
| Rocky | Unpredictable kicks, disc damage, no tombstone. |
| Dry grass | Long roll and skip. |
| Tall grass | Disc stops dead, find-disc roll needed. |
| Ice | Huge slide and skip, no tombstone, slipping risk when throwing from it. |
| *(suggestion)* Mud | Tombstones easily, no roll. |
| *(suggestion)* Sand/bunker | Disc stops, awkward stance. |
| *(suggestion)* Concrete/path | Big skips, disc wear. |
