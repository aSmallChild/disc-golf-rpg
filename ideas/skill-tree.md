# Skill Tree

- Unlike Fallout, skills are tied to a tree: some skills unlock other skills.
- Two skill kinds:
  - **Chance skills** improve success odds.
  - **Unlock skills** grant new abilities (shot types, actions).
- Gear is heavily tied to skills. Gear boosts chances, and a higher related skill means fewer negatives and bigger benefits.
- Progression paths will be designed later after the ideas are tagged and categorised.
