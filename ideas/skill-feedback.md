# Skill Feedback

Whenever a skill is used, the game tells the player why it succeeded or failed.

*(suggestion)* Show a breakdown of the roll, for example:
```
Spike hyzer over pine: 62%
  +30 Throwing skill
  +8  Chalk bag (grip)
  -12 Headwind 15 km/h
  -6  Wet grip (rain)
  +2  Luck
Rolled 71 -> FAIL: wet release, disc clipped the top branch.
```
- Pair the numbers with a short flavour line, keeping the tone light.
- The top one or two contributing factors decide which flavour line is picked.
