# Character Stats

Fallout SPECIAL style, but disc-golf appropriate. Stats feed into skills, and skills sit in a tree.

*(suggestion)* Candidate stats:
| Stat | Replaces | Affects |
|---|---|---|
| **Arm** | Strength | Distance, wind punch-through |
| **Touch** | Agility/Dexterity | Accuracy, putting, release consistency |
| **Read** | Perception | Wind reading, line spotting, finding lost discs |
| **Nerve** | Endurance | Composure under pressure, resisting tilt |
| **Stamina** | Endurance | Performance late in rounds and tournaments |
| **Swagger** | Charisma | Sponsors, shop prices, crowd, multiplayer banter |
| **Disc IQ** | Intelligence | Skill points per level, disc selection insight |
| **Luck** | Luck | Everything, a little (see `luck.md`) |

A possible acronym to play with later: decide during review.
