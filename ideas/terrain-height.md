# Terrain Height and Obstacles

Top-down view, but the game needs height: hills, slopes, and obstacle heights (trees, bushes, walls).

- Slopes make the disc roll away (tombstone prevents this).
- Tall obstacles need a high arc (spike hyzer) or an overhand shot.
- Elevated tees add distance, and downhill baskets are harder to stop near.

See `docs/plan.md` for technical representation options.
