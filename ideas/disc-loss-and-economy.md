# Disc Loss and Economy

- Discs can be lost permanently.
- No discs and no money means game over.

*(suggestions)*
- Earn money from tournament winnings, sponsors (Swagger), and side bets.
- Spend money on discs, gear, chalk refills, entry fees, and repairs.
- Lost-and-found: luck-based chance a lost disc gets returned later.
- Disc wear: discs get beat in over time and their flight changes (understable).
