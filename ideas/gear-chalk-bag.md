# Gear: Chalk Bag

User's notes:
- Useful for wind reads and grip.
- Helps manage anger by clapping the bag after a bad putt.
- Small increase to grip lock chance.
- Boosts spin, which improves range.
- Higher skill makes the negatives less likely and the benefits bigger.
- Low luck increases the chance that the bag breaks and blinds the player, so they need a time extension (see `skill-scoring.md`, `shot-clock.md`).

*(suggestions)* Benefits:
- **Wind read**: tossing chalk shows a dust puff that reveals wind direction and strength. Higher skill gives more precise readings.
- **Grip**: reduces the grip penalty from rain, sweat, or snow.
- **Spin**: + range and + stability (less turnover in headwind).
- **Calm clap**: reduces tilt after a bad shot. Limited uses per round.

*(suggestions)* Negatives at low skill:
- Over-chalking: slippery release, small accuracy penalty.
- Chalk cloud in the face: brief Read penalty if you clap upwind.
- Clumped chalk in rain: becomes useless or makes grip worse.
- Running out: bags have limited charges and need refills from the shop.
