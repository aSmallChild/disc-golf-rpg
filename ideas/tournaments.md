# Tournaments

The game will have tournaments.

*(suggestions)*
- Tiers: local weekly, then regional, then pro tour.
- Entry fees and prize money feed the economy.
- Multi-round events, where Stamina matters.
- Special rules per event: e.g. one-disc challenge, night glow round, ice bowl.
- *(suggestion)* **Bot balance**: bots take risks at a rate that matches their skill, so a player who only plays safe finishes mid-pack, not first. This is what keeps "always lay up to stay calm" from being a winning strategy (`nerve-meter.md`).
