# Gear: Sunglasses

## User's notes
- Improve accuracy in the sun.
- Decrease accuracy at night.
- Calm nerves because people can't see your face.

## Suggestions
- *(suggestion)* Polarised lenses: reduce glare off water, making shots over water less nervy and helping find discs in shallow water.
- *(suggestion)* Make rain worse (wet lenses): a small accuracy penalty, unless you have a towel.
- *(suggestion)* Cheap pair: lowers expectation slightly. Expensive designer pair: raises expectation, but calms nerves more.
- *(suggestion)* Can fall off on a hard throw at low skill (a short shot-clock delay to pick them up).
