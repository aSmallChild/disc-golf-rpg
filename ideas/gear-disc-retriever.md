# Gear: Telescopic Disc Retrieval Stick

- Improves the chance of retrieving a disc.
- A failed retrieval means the disc is lost.
- No discs and no money means **game over**.
- An important skill that players may not think to invest in. This should be a "lesson learned" moment.

*(suggestions)*
- Retrieval difficulty depends on location: shallow water, deep water, a tree canopy, thick bush, a cliff ledge.
- Higher skill brings longer reach, fewer snapped sticks, and the ability to retrieve from deep water or high branches.
- Low skill negatives: the stick breaks, you fall into the water (soaked, grip penalty), or you knock the disc further in.
- Alternatives without the stick: wading (stamina cost), climbing (risky), paying a local kid (money), or luck (a stranger returns it later).
