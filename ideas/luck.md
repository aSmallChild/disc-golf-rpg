# Luck

The most important stat.
- **Luck attribute**: a small passive influence on everything.
- **Luck skill**: a concentrated application of luck toward a chosen outcome. It can achieve things that would otherwise be locked, such as **flukes**.

*(suggestions)*
- Spend a limited "luck charge" per round to push a single roll.
- Fluke examples: tree kick into the basket, skip off water, disc lands on top of the basket and drops in, a squirrel moves your disc.
- Bad luck backfire at low skill: the fluke goes the wrong way.
- The feedback should name luck explicitly: "Lucky! A branch deflected your disc into the chains."
